Transind Holding

Transind Holding is a group of companies proud of constant growth since its foundation, having more than 1500 employees. It is the company owned by Sheikh Abdulrahman bin Mohamed bin Jabor Al-Thani and his family members.
In several years, their global approach, which centered entirely on customer needs, has put them in the forefront of progress in the Food Trading segment and now in the hospitality, industry and ports trading and facility management. The reasons for the success of Transind Holding can be explained by a series of unique talents and skills.
A passion for the profession which allows us to bring highly reliable and customer worldwide trusted products; a strong motivation by all their business partners who are aware that Transind Holding success directly translates into their own success. Profit Holding

Profit Holding is a well reputed conglomerate based in Qatar with business activities in a wide range of sectors and industries. Founded in 2008 by prominent Qatari businessmen, Sh. Fahad Bin Mohamed bin Jabor Al Thani and Sh. Abdul Rahman Bin Mohamed bin Jabor Al Thani, Profit Holding has since then diversified its core activities to include car rental & vehicle leasing, limousine, taxi services, catering, Auto repair, security services, advertising, facilities management, food & beverage and security equipment & services. Profit Holding aspires to play a pivotal role in the economic growth of the State of Qatar by fostering mutually beneficial partnerships, adopting best operational practices and maximizing value for its stakeholders.
