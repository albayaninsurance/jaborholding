LEADER BEHIND THE SUCCESS

Sheikh Jabor bin Abdulrahman bin Mohamed bin Jabor Al-Thani

Sheikh Jabor bin Abdulrahman bin Mohamed bin Jabor Al-Thani, The Chairman of Jabor Holding, a prominent young Qatari business person has important role in different types of businesses. He is one of the most influential and strongly active characters in various sectors of the economy in Qatar and the GCC area. He also holds a major place in the Qatar Investment Authority.
Sheikh Jabor is one of the biggest entrepreneurs in the insurance sector and retail distribution in the country and the region, and in one of the largest Retail distributions in Qatar; and that is Transind Holding, which is the sole distributor of many of the well-known international and local FMCG brands.
Sheikh Jabor is also a main shareholder of Doha Bank, one of the largest commercial banks in the State of Qatar, which has been consistently registering a strong growth during the last decade with a participative leadership philosophy.
Sheikh Jabor is also a Board Member at Aamal Company since February 2017, Vice Chairman and Managing Director of Transind Group since 2004, Founder and Managing Director of Al-Bayan Insurance Broker since 2011, Managing Director of Al Arabi Sports Club, and Managing Director of Lebanese Intermediate Company since 2018.
Sheikh Jabor Holds a Bachelor of Business Administration from European University – Geneva, Switzerland in 2009, Certified Financial Analyst from American Academy of Financial Management in 2007 and Holds Professional Diploma in Financial Management and Banking from The Arab Academy for Banking and Financial Sciences in 2006.
