Global Association of Risk Professionals®

The Leading Risk Management Association

GARP® is the world's top professional association for risk managers, dedicated to the advancement of the profession through education, research, and the promotion of best practices.

Become a Member

Financial Risk Manager (FRM®)

The globally recognized standard for those who manage financial risk. Be part of an elite network of 100,000 FRM-Certified Professionals that includes employees at the world’s top banks, asset management firms, consulting firms, and regulators.

FRM Overview

Sustainability and Climate Risk (SCR®)

From entry-level to executive, across industries such as banking, fintech, hospitality, and more, SCR Certificate Holders are leading their companies with solutions to today's top issues in climate change and sustainability.  

SCR Overview

Risk and AI (RAI™)

Your gateway to understanding artificial intelligence and the potential risks, regulations, and ethical considerations 
associated with its use. Our RAI Certificate offers the knowledge you need to harness the transformative power of AI and machine learning (ML), and ensure AI is deployed responsibly within your organization.

Supporting the Risk Community

Learn about GARP's partnerships, outreach efforts, and initiatives connecting
risk managers worldwide, from students to senior professionals.

  • Find out how GARP is helping colleges and universities meet the demands of the global financial industry.

    University Outreach

    Our university outreach activities help foster collaborations between academia and the risk profession. At the intersection of knowledge and practice, we aim to propel innovation, elevate education, and inspire the next generation of risk managers. 

    Learn More

  • Learn about the benefits of a corporate partnership with GARP.

    Corporate Outreach

    GARP is committed to helping organizations with creating a culture of risk awareness® through in-house learning events as well as opportunities for firms to allocate training and development budgets toward the engagement of their teams in a relevant GARP educational program.

    Learn More

  • Exchange ideas and facilitate dialogues with peer practitioners, regulators, and academic experts.

    Events and Networking

    GARP Events facilitate dialogues on industry trends, opportunities, and challenges, while offering professionals the opportunity to connect with peer practitioners, regulators, and academic experts at our Climate and Nature Risk Symposium, Financial Risk Symposium, local Chapter events and more.

    See All Events

  • Learn, share best practices, and join discussions on current and emerging risk issues.

    GARP Forums

    From the GARP Global Risk Forum® to our Buy Side Risk Managers Forum, our high-level forums convene chief risk officers from the largest global asset management firms for learning, sharing best practices, and actionable discussions of current and emerging risk issues.
